1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY
================================================================================
HEALESTHETIC is an enterprise-grade multi-branch clinical management platform 
built specifically for modern healthcare facilities combining General Outpatient 
Medicine with Advanced Dermatology, Aesthetic Daycare, Trichology, and In-House 
Diagnostics/Dispensary operations.

Key System Principles:
----------------------
1. Single Shared Patient Identity: Every patient has one unique OP Number 
   (e.g., A00373) across all branches, departments, and applications.
2. Distinct Encounters: Every visit generates a unique Encounter ID (e.g., ENC-01001) 
   categorized by type: CONSULTATION, DIRECT_PHARMACY, DIRECT_LAB, DIRECT_NURSING, 
   or IMAGING.
3. Multi-Department Parallel Routing: A doctor can simultaneously order medicines, 
   blood tests, imaging scans, and nursing procedures in a single click without 
   departmental bottlenecks.
4. "Returned with Results" Closed Loop: When diagnostic tests are verified and 
   released, the patient automatically returns to the doctor's dedicated review tab.
5. Patient Portal Privacy Shield: Unverified lab or imaging reports are strictly 
   hidden from the patient portal until formally verified and released by specialists.

3. COMPLETE PATIENT CLINICAL JOURNEY (STEP-BY-STEP FLOW)
================================================================================

Below is the exact chronological sequence of how a patient moves through the 
clinic and software from the moment they walk through the front door:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: RECEPTION DESK INTAKE (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Receptionist (username: reception)
Location: Core App -> "Patient Registration" or "Appointments"

1. Patient arrives at clinic reception.
2. Receptionist selects the registration mode:
   A. "Primary Details Intake" (Fast-Track - Recommended for busy OPD):
      - Only requires: Full Name + Date of Birth (Age auto-calculates) + Phone.
      - Receptionist assigns the consulting Doctor (e.g., Dr. Ananya Nair).
      - Clicking "Register Patient" instantly creates an OP Number (e.g., A00373), 
        starts Encounter ENC-01001, and queues the patient directly to the Nurse 
        Station for vital signs intake.
   B. "Full HMS Registration" (Detailed aesthetic/surgical intake):
      - Allows entering detailed residential address, emergency contact, referral 
        source (Instagram, Google, Walk-in), and photo upload.
3. Reception prints or issues the OP Card / Token Number.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: NURSE STATION TRIAGE & VITALS INTAKE (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Staff Nurse (username: nurse)
Location: Core App -> "Nurse Station" or "Clinical Desk"

1. Nurse calls patient to the intake desk.
2. Nurse searches or selects the patient from the "Arrived - Awaiting Triage" queue.
3. Nurse enters vital statistics:
   - Blood Pressure (Systolic / Diastolic mmHg)
   - Pulse / Heart Rate (bpm)
   - Temperature (°F) & Oxygen Saturation (SpO2 %)
   - Height (cm) & Weight (kg) -> System auto-calculates BMI & Category
   - Confirms Blood Group (A+, B+, O+, AB+, etc.)
   - Enters Known Drug Allergies (e.g., Penicillin, Sulfa, NSAIDs)
4. Nurse clicks "Submit & Send to Doctor".
5. The patient's status switches to "Ready for Doctor" and appears immediately in 
   the Doctor's consultation room queue.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: DOCTOR WORKBENCH CONSULTATION (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Doctor (username: dr.ananya or dr.rahul)
Location: Core App -> "Doctor Workbench"

1. Doctor sits at consultation desk and selects their assigned Room / Branch.
2. In the left panel, Doctor clicks on the patient in the "Consultation Queue".
3. System loads complete clinical context:
   - Verified Nurse Vitals banner highlighted in green (Recorded by Staff Nurse).
   - Patient demographics, Blood Group, Age, and active allergies.
   - Previous visit history, past prescriptions, and aesthetic treatment logs.
4. Doctor enters clinical notes:
   - Chief Complaints (e.g., "Inflammatory acne flare-up on cheeks for 3 weeks").
   - Clinical Examination findings.
   - Provisional / Final Diagnosis (e.g., "Grade III Acne Vulgaris").
   - Treatment Plan & Patient Instructions.
5. For Aesthetic Procedures: Doctor can click the Face/Body canvas to drop pins 
   marking injection points (Botox/Filler units) or laser settings (Fluence, Pulse).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: MULTI-DEPARTMENT SIMULTANEOUS ORDERING (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Doctor
Location: Inside "Doctor Workbench" -> Order Tabs

In a single consultation, the doctor can simultaneously route orders to 4 separate 
departments without opening separate software modules:

A. Pharmacy Order (Rx):
   - Type medicine name (e.g., "Doxt-SL 100mg" or "UV-Doux Sunscreen Gel").
   - Select Dosage Form, Frequency (1-0-1), Timing (After Food), and Duration (14 days).
   - Enter special instructions (e.g., "Take with full glass of water").
   - Prescribed items route live to Pharmacy Dispensary (Port 3002).

B. Laboratory Diagnostics Order:
   - Check required tests: Complete Blood Count (CBC), Liver Function Test (LFT), 
     HbA1c, Serum Ferritin, etc.
   - Orders route live to Laboratory Station.

C. Imaging & Radiology Order:
   - Select scan modality & procedure: Chest X-Ray PA View, Ultrasound Abdomen, 
     Brain MRI, or CT Scan.
   - Orders route live to Radiology Department.

D. Nursing & Daycare Procedure Order:
   - Prescribe in-clinic treatments: Salicylic Chemical Peel, IV Infusion, 
     Intralesional Steroid Injection, or Wound Dressing.
   - Orders route live to Nursing Treatment Lounge.

PRINTING PRESCRIPTIONS:
- Doctor clicks "Print Prescription".
- Letterhead outputs exact official branding: "HEALESTHETIC CLINIC & ADVANCED 
  AESTHETIC CARE" with Doctor Medical Registration No, Address, and Helpline.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: LABORATORY STATION EXECUTION (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Lab Technician (username: labtech)
Location: Core App -> "Laboratory"

1. Patient arrives at sample collection counter.
2. Lab tech views order LAB-CBC, collects EDTA blood sample, and records sample barcode.
3. Test processing:
   - Technician enters observed parameters (e.g., Hemoglobin: 11.2 g/dL, WBC: 8,400).
   - System flags abnormal values in bold red.
4. Verification & Release:
   - Pathologist reviews draft findings.
   - Pathologist clicks "Verify & Release Report".
   - Status changes to RELEASED.
   - An unbilled pending charge (e.g., ₹450) is automatically queued for billing.
   - The verified report is now instantly accessible in the Doctor's "Returned with 
     Results" queue and Patient Web Portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: RADIOLOGY & IMAGING EXECUTION (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Radiologist (username: radiologist)
Location: Core App -> "Imaging & Radiology"

1. Radiologist views active orders (e.g., Chest X-Ray PA View).
2. Scan workflow:
   - SCHEDULED -> IN_PROGRESS -> REPORT_READY.
3. Radiologist types clinical impressions (e.g., "Normal cardiac silhouette; clear lung fields").
4. Radiologist verifies scan and releases report.
5. Unbilled pending charge is queued for cashier; report is routed to Doctor and Patient.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: PHARMACY DISPENSARY & OTC COUNTER (PORT 3002)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Pharmacist (username: pharmacist)
Location: Pharmacy OS App -> "Rx Dispensing Queue" & "Direct Walk-In"

Scenario A: Doctor Prescription Fulfillment (Rx Queue):
1. Pharmacist opens "Incoming Doctor Prescriptions".
2. System displays prescribed medicines, dosage, and remaining quantities.
3. System applies FEFO (First-Expiry, First-Out) rule to pull the earliest expiring batch.
4. Supports Partial Dispensing: If patient wants only 7 days instead of 14 days, 
   pharmacist adjusts quantity; system retains remaining balance for future visits.
5. If Schedule H1 drug is dispensed: mandatory compliance modal prompts for Doctor 
   Registration No. and logs entry into the Schedule H1 Statutory Register.
6. Pharmacist clicks "Dispense". Stock is automatically deducted from inventory, 
   and a separate GST-compliant Pharmacy Bill (e.g., PHM-01001) is generated.

Scenario B: Direct Walk-In Sale (No Doctor Consultation):
1. A customer walks in solely to buy sunscreen, moisturizers, or OTC medicines.
2. Pharmacist clicks "+ Direct Walk-In Sale" button.
3. Search patient or type Name + DOB for quick registration.
4. Add items to cart; click "Complete Walk-In Sale & Generate Bill".
5. Creates a DIRECT_PHARMACY encounter and separate bill WITHOUT charging any doctor 
   consultation fee.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8: DOCTOR REVIEW VIA "RETURNED WITH RESULTS" (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Doctor
Location: Doctor Workbench -> "Returned with Results" Tab

1. After lab/imaging tests are released, the patient automatically appears in the 
   Doctor's "Returned with Results" tab with an active notification badge.
2. Doctor clicks patient:
   - System displays verified lab parameters, abnormal indicators, and scan findings.
3. Doctor discusses results with patient, adjusts medication if necessary, and advises follow-up.
4. Doctor clicks "Complete Consultation & Close Encounter".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9: CENTRAL BILLING & CASHIER POS (PORT 3000)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Cashier (username: cashier)
Location: Core App -> "Billing & Cashier"

1. Cashier opens Billing Desk and selects patient.
2. Intelligent Unbilled Charges Sync:
   - System automatically aggregates all completed services (Consultation Fee, 
     CBC Blood Test, Chest X-Ray, Nursing Chemical Peel).
3. Cashier applies any authorized discount or promotional package redemption.
4. Cashier selects payment mode: Cash, Card (POS), UPI (GooglePay / PhonePe).
5. Click "Collect Payment & Print Invoice":
   - Produces official tax invoice showing CGST + SGST breakdown and zero balance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 10: PATIENT SELF-SERVICE WEB PORTAL (PORT 3003)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performed by: Patient
Location: Patient Portal App (https://healh-patient.netlify.app or Port 3003)

1. Patient opens portal from their smartphone or home browser.
2. Enters registered mobile number (e.g., 9876543210).
3. Enters 6-digit OTP received via SMS (or Dev OTP displayed during testing).
4. Patient Dashboard Features:
   - Profile card with OP No, Blood Group, and Allergies.
   - Visit Timeline: History of all clinic visits and encounters.
   - Prescriptions: Digital list of medicines, dosage, and doctor instructions.
   - Lab Reports: Download official PDF reports for released lab tests.
   - Imaging Reports: View radiologist findings and scan documentation.
   - Invoices: View past payment receipts and balance status.

4. DOCTOR WORKBENCH IN-DEPTH HOW-TO GUIDE
================================================================================

How to perform daily clinical tasks quickly:

1. How to switch between waiting patients and test returnees:
   - Left Sidebar Tab 1: "Active Consultation Queue" (New patients waiting to be seen).
   - Left Sidebar Tab 2: "Returned with Results" (Patients whose lab/X-ray tests are done).

2. How to check Nurse Vitals:
   - Look at the top green banner in the patient chart.
   - Check BP, Pulse, SpO2, BMI, and Blood Group verified by the nurse.

3. How to write a fast prescription:
   - In Rx tab, type the first 3 letters of brand or salt name.
   - Select frequency: 1-0-1 (Morning & Night), 1-0-0 (Morning only), 0-0-1 (Night only), 
     or SOS (As needed).
   - Select duration in days (e.g., 5, 7, 14, 30).
   - Quantity auto-calculates (e.g., 1-0-1 for 7 days = 14 tablets).

4. How to order a Lab Test:
   - In Lab tab, check the box next to CBC, LFT, or HbA1c, or search custom tests.
   - Add clinical notes for the technician (e.g., "Fasting sample required").

5. How to order an X-Ray / MRI / USG:
   - In Imaging tab, pick Modality (X-Ray, MRI, Ultrasound, CT).
   - Select body part / view (e.g., Chest PA, USG Abdomen).

6. How to finish a consultation:
   - Once all notes and orders are written, click "Finalize Consultation".
   - Click "Print Prescription" to generate the signed clinical letterhead.

5. AESTHETIC PROCEDURES, PACKAGES & SESSIONS
================================================================================
For aesthetic and dermatology treatments, HEALESTHETIC provides multi-session 
package tracking:

1. Selling a Package:
   - Reception or Billing sells a package (e.g., "Laser Hair Reduction - 6 Sessions" 
     for ₹15,000).
   - Package validity is locked (e.g., 12 months).

2. Redeeming a Session:
   - Doctor or Aesthetician selects patient package tab.
   - System shows: "Sessions Completed: 2 / 6 — 4 Sessions Remaining".
   - Doctor logs session details: Spot Size (12mm), Fluence (24 J/cm²), Pulse Width (30ms).
   - Doctor clicks "Redeem Session 3".
   - Session counter increments to 3/6; no billing charge is created since the 
     package was prepaid.

6. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & MEDICO-LEGAL RULES
================================================================================
The software enforces Indian clinical, pharmaceutical, and tax standards:

1. Schedule H1 Mandatory Register:
   - For restricted antibiotics (e.g., Doxycycline, Cefixime) and psychotropics, 
     the pharmacy module locks dispensing until Patient Aadhaar/ID, Doctor TCMC 
     Registration Number, and clinical indication are recorded.
   - Generates statutory Form 35 register ready for drug inspector audits.

2. FEFO Expiry Protection:
   - System prevents dispensing newer stock if an earlier batch of the same drug 
     is available and unexpired.
   - Batches within 30 days of expiry are highlighted in amber; expired batches 
     are locked from dispensing entirely.

3. Two-Tier Diagnostic Verification:
   - Laboratory and imaging results remain in "DRAFT" state until explicitly verified 
     by a pathologist or radiologist.
   - Draft reports are strictly hidden from patient view to prevent misinterpretation 
     before clinical correlation.

4. Hybrid GST Split:
   - Medical consultations, doctor fees, and inpatient nursing care: 0% GST (Exempt).
   - Pharmaceutical drugs & medical devices: 5% or 12% GST (HSN 3004).
   - Cosmetic skincare & aesthetic retail products: 18% or 28% GST (HSN 3304).
   - System splits CGST/SGST automatically on every invoice.

7. SYSTEM TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ
================================================================================

Q1: The patient says they cannot see their lab test in the Patient Portal?
A: Check with the lab technician. Reports only appear in the Patient Portal after 
   the status is marked as "RELEASED" by the pathologist.

Q2: How do we start the software in the morning on clinic computers?
A: Double-click "start-all.bat" on the desktop. It opens 5 terminal windows and 
   starts the Backend (8000), Core App (3000), HRMS (3001), Pharmacy (3002), 
   and Patient Portal (3003).

Q3: How do we stop all services in the evening?
A: Double-click "stop-all.bat" on the desktop. It safely shuts down all 5 services.

Q4: What if a patient loses their prescription paper?
A: Doctor or Receptionist can re-open the patient chart, click on the encounter date, 
   and click "Print Prescription" to reprint anytime with the exact original timestamp.

Q5: Can a patient buy skincare without seeing a doctor?
A: Yes. The pharmacist uses the "+ Direct Walk-In Sale" button in Port 3002. It 
   generates a Pharmacy bill without creating any consultation charge.
