Stage 01

Reception Desk — Patient Arrival & Fast-Track Intake

Operator: Front Desk Receptionist
Where to go in software Core App → Menu → "Patient Registration"
What the Receptionist Does:
  1. Selects Primary Details Intake (Reception → Nurse Queue).
  2. Enters Full Name (e.g. Fathima Zahra).
  3. Picks Date of Birth — the software automatically calculates and shows their exact age in years!
  4. Enters optional Phone Number and selects the Consulting Doctor.
  5. Clicks the green "Register Patient & Send to Nurse Queue" button.
What to Check Next:

A permanent OP Number (e.g. A00373) is generated. An active Encounter is started, and the patient immediately appears on the Nurse Station screen.

Stage 02

Nurse Station — Pre-Doctor Clinical Intake & Vitals

Operator: Staff Nurse
Where to go in software Core App → Menu → "Nurse Station" or "Appointments Queue"
What the Nurse Does:
  1. Nurse calls patient to the triage desk from the "Arrived — Awaiting Vitals" list.
  2. Measures and logs vital signs:
    • Blood Pressure: Systolic / Diastolic (e.g. 118/76 mmHg)
    • Pulse & SpO2: Heart rate and Oxygen level
    • Height & Weight: System automatically calculates BMI (Body Mass Index)
    • Blood Group: O+, A+, B+, AB+
    • Known Drug Allergies: e.g. Penicillin or None
  3. Nurse clicks "Submit Vitals & Send to Doctor".
What to Check Next:

Patient status turns to "Ready for Doctor". The patient is sent to sit outside Doctor Consultation Room.

https://healh-software.netlify.app/reception/queue
Live Screenshot: Nurse queue showing arrived patients awaiting vitals intake.
Stage 03

Doctor Consultation Room — Clinical Examination & Notes

Operator: Consulting Doctor
Where to go in software Core App → Menu → "Doctor Workbench"
What the Doctor Sees & Does:
  1. Doctor clicks on patient in the left "Consultation Queue".
  2. Green Verified Vitals Banner: Shows BP, BMI, Blood Group, and Nurse Name at the very top of the chart.
  3. Doctor writes:
    • Chief Complaints: e.g. "Acne on cheeks and jawline for 3 weeks"
    • Clinical Findings & Examination
    • Provisional / Final Diagnosis: e.g. "Grade III Inflammatory Acne"
  4. Aesthetic Charting: For skin/hair sessions, doctor drops interactive pins on the 2D anatomical face canvas.
What to Check Next:

Doctor proceeds to the bottom order tabs to prescribe medicines or order diagnostics (Stage 4).

https://healh-software.netlify.app/clinical/doctor

Stage 04

Multi-Department Orders — Simultaneous Pharmacy, Lab & Scans

Operator: Consulting Doctor
1. Pharmacy Order (Rx)

Type medicine name, select frequency (1-0-1), duration in days, and food instructions. Routes directly to Pharmacy Dispensary.

2. Laboratory Blood Tests

Tick blood tests: Complete Blood Count (CBC), Liver Function Test (LFT), Lipid, HbA1c. Routes directly to Diagnostic Lab.

3. Radiology Scans

Select scan modality: Chest X-Ray, Ultrasound Abdomen, Brain MRI. Routes directly to Imaging & Radiology.

4. Nursing Procedures

Order in-clinic treatments: Chemical Peel, Laser session, IV Drip, or Intralesional Injection. Routes to Procedure Lounge.

Printed Prescription Header Outputs exact branding: HEALESTHETIC CLINIC & ADVANCED AESTHETIC CARE with Medical Reg No. and Helpline.

Stage 07

Results Return — Doctor Reviews Reports & Closes Visit

Operator: Consulting Doctor
Where to go in software Doctor Workbench → Click "Returned with Results" tab
How the Closed-Loop Review Works:
  1. Doctor does not need to search for the patient again.
  2. As soon as the lab or radiologist clicks "Release", the patient pops up in the "Returned with Results" tab with a live badge.
  3. Doctor clicks patient to review verified blood test values and radiologist scan notes side-by-side.
  4. Doctor writes final advice, adjusts medication if necessary, and tells patient next steps.
  5. Doctor clicks "Complete Consultation & Close Visit".
What to Check Next:

Patient is sent to the Cashier for payment collection.

Stage 08

Central Billing Desk — Automated Charge Aggregation & GST POS

Operator: Billing Cashier
Where to go in software Core App → Menu → "Billing & Cashier"
Zero Missed Charges:
  1. Cashier selects the patient name or OP Number.
  2. Auto-Pulled Pending Charges: The system automatically aggregates every completed service:
    • Doctor Consultation Fee
    • Laboratory Blood Tests (CBC, LFT)
    • Radiology Scans (X-Ray, MRI)
    • Daycare Nursing Procedures
  3. Cashier selects payment method: Cash, Card (POS), or UPI (GPay / PhonePe).
  4. Clicks "Collect Payment & Print GST Invoice".
What to Check Next:

Patient receives printed GST invoice. Patient balance becomes ₹0.00.
