8-Stage Clinical Patient Journey

Standard Operating Procedure

Stage 01

Reception Desk — Fast-Track Patient Registration

Operator: Front Desk Receptionist
Menu Location Core App → "Patient Registration"
Receptionist Workflow:
  1. Selects Primary Details Intake (Reception → Nurse Queue).
  2. Enters Full Name (e.g. Fathima Zahra).
  3. Selects Date of Birth — system calculates exact age automatically!
  4. Assigns consulting Doctor (e.g. Dr. Ananya Nair).
  5. Clicks "Register Patient & Send to Nurse Queue".
Result: Permanent OP Number (e.g. A00373) is generated; patient is instantly routed to Nurse Station.
/reception/register
Stage 02

Nurse Station — Pre-Doctor Intake & Vital Signs

Operator: Staff Nurse
Menu Location Core App → "Nurse Station" or "Appointments"
Nurse Workflow:
  1. Calls patient from "Awaiting Vitals" queue.
  2. Logs vital signs:
    • Blood Pressure (Systolic / Diastolic)
    • Pulse Rate & Oxygen SpO2 %
    • Height & Weight → System calculates BMI category
    • Blood Group (A+, B+, O+, AB+) & Known Allergies
  3. Clicks "Submit Vitals & Send to Doctor".
Result: Patient status becomes "Ready for Doctor" and appears in doctor room queue.
/reception/queue (Nurse Intake)
Stage 03

Doctor Room — Consultation & Clinical Examination

Operator: Consulting Doctor
Menu Location Core App → "Doctor Workbench"
Doctor Workflow:
  1. Clicks patient in left "Consultation Queue".
  2. Verified Green Banner: Top banner shows nurse's recorded BP, BMI, Blood Group, and nurse name.
  3. Enters Chief Complaints, Examination, Diagnosis, and Treatment Plan.
  4. For skin/laser treatments, drops pins on 2D anatomical canvas to mark units/fluence.
Result: Complete clinical documentation saved; doctor writes multi-department orders next.
/clinical/doctor
Stage 04

Multi-Department Orders — 1-Click Parallel Routing

Operator: Consulting Doctor

From the bottom tabs of the Doctor Workbench, the doctor generates parallel departmental orders in a single consultation:

Pharmacy Rx Medicines, dosage (1-0-1), duration & food timing. Routes to Dispensary.
Blood Tests Tick CBC, LFT, Lipid, HbA1c. Routes directly to Pathology Laboratory.
Radiology Scans Select Chest X-Ray, USG Abdomen, MRI Brain. Routes to Imaging Room.
Daycare Care Order Chemical Peel, IV Infusion, Wound Dressing. Routes to Nurse Lounge.
Prescription Letterhead: Clicking "Print Prescription" outputs branded header HEALESTHETIC CLINIC & ADVANCED AESTHETIC CARE with doctor license number and clinic phone.
Stage 05

Diagnostics Execution — Pathology Lab & Radiology

Operators: Lab Tech & Radiologist
Pathology Lab (/clinical/lab)

Tech draws sample, inputs observed values; abnormal flags display automatically. Pathologist clicks "Release Report".

/clinical/lab
Radiology & Scans (/clinical/imaging)

Radiologist conducts X-Ray/MRI/USG, logs clinical impressions, and clicks "Verify Scan". Test charges queue for cashier.

/clinical/imaging
Stage 06

Pharmacy Counter — Doctor Rx Queue & Direct Walk-In Sales

Operator: Chief Pharmacist
1. Doctor Prescription Queue (FEFO)

Dispenses against doctor orders. Pulls earliest expiring batch automatically. Schedule H1 drugs prompt statutory doctor reg ID lock.

/pharmacy/dispensary
2. Direct Walk-In OTC Skincare Sales

For retail sunscreen or skincare sales without a doctor consultation, click "+ Direct Walk-In Sale" to bill with 0 consultation charges.

Direct Walk-In Modal
Stage 07

Closed-Loop Results Return — Doctor Reviews Reports & Closes Visit

Operator: Consulting Doctor
Menu Location Doctor Workbench → "Returned with Results" Tab
Review Workflow:
  1. Doctor does not re-search patient.
  2. When lab/radiology releases reports, patient appears in "Returned with Results" tab with live badge.
  3. Doctor reviews verified blood test values and scan impressions.
  4. Doctor provides final advice, adjusts medicines if needed, and clicks "Complete Consultation & Close Visit".
Result: Clinical visit is marked COMPLETED; patient proceeds to cashier for payment.
/clinical/doctor (Returned with Results)
Stage 08

Central Billing Desk — Automated Charge Aggregation & GST POS

Operator: Billing Cashier
Menu Location Core App → "Billing & Cashier"
Zero Missed Charges:
  1. Cashier selects patient.
  2. Auto-Aggregated Charges: System automatically pulls all completed services:
    • Doctor Consultation Fee
    • Laboratory Tests (CBC, LFT)
    • Radiology Scans (X-Ray, MRI)
    • Nursing Daycare Procedures
  3. Cashier collects via Cash, Card (POS), or UPI.
  4. Prints official GST tax invoice; balance sets to ₹0.00.
/billing/pos
